What to check before you order

  1. Match the listing and variant

    Save the listing ID, seller link, color and size. If HipobuyFind shows an ACBuy option under Buy Link, open it and compare the translated title, photos and selected variant before paying.

  2. Compare measurements and QC photos

    For shoes, compare the insole length with a pair that fits. For tops, measure a similar garment flat and compare chest width, body length and sleeves. If warehouse quality-control photos are available, look for the selected color, size label, stitching and visible damage before approving shipment.

  3. Estimate the delivered total

    Add the item price, domestic shipping, agent fees, international shipping and any applicable import charges. Check whether your shipping line bills by weight or parcel dimensions, especially if you keep shoe boxes.

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQs

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

It is a catalog used to shortlist products before checking whether they can be ordered through ACBuy. This independent guide pulls its featured listings from HipobuyFind, where you can review the source and available purchasing options.

Are the product images warehouse QC photos?

The cards show images from the source listings. When your agent offers warehouse QC, review the photos attached to your specific order to check the actual item and variant before shipment.

What if a link opens a different item or shows no stock?

Compare the listing ID, name and selected variant on the source page. If the listing changed or became unavailable, search HipobuyFind again and verify the replacement’s seller link, price and measurements before ordering.
